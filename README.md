# Retcon

The world has been overrun by a corrupt government and their destructive machines. As humanity teeters on the brink of extinction, you travel back to the past with one last chance to change history. Guided only by fragmented notes you wrote on your phone in the future, you must uncover the truth, outmaneuver the regime, and prevent the rise of the machines before the world falls into ruin.

## Mechanics

### Action Side-Scrolling Gameplay

Traverse urban and industrial areas in a side-scrolling action format. Face enemies, overcome obstacles, and progress through controlled zones as tensions rise around the growing machine threat.

### Phone "Spoiler" System

You carry a phone filled with notes written by your future self. In certain areas, the phone reveals information relevant to that location, such as warnings, observations, or fragments of events yet to occur. These notes provide guidance without fully revealing what lies ahead.
